**Ace’s Gameday Atmosphere 2.1.38**
Changes since public release 2.1.11.

New Dynasty crowds can give visiting fans several nearby blocks. Added visiting cheers at home stadiums need at least 20% visiting support among fans present. Spring games use one school's crowd, and saved matchup plans carry forward.

Close MMC and the game, then copy GamedayAtmosphere.dll from the standalone ZIP into MMC’s Plugins folder. The ZIP includes the full feature list and an illustrated player guide.
