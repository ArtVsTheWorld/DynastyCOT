# Ace’s Gameday Atmosphere 2.1.38

## Watch the stands fill and empty

Open **Dynasty Central > User Settings > Gameday Atmosphere**, or **Pause > Options > Settings** and move right past Auto-Subs. Set up the crowd before starting a new game. The mod works on its own in supported offline Dynasty games and basic offline Play Now.

- **Who comes to the game?** A fresh regular-season Dynasty home date looks at how many fans usually follow each school, the stadium, recent seasons, school prestige, rank, opponent and rivalry. Time, weather and a roof can matter. Custom programs can draw on similar schools. A bigger stadium can have more empty seats. Some showcase rivalries have special turnout and earlier arrivals.
- **Who is in a seat now?** Fans can arrive during the first quarter, take a break near halftime and return early in the third. Home and visiting fans can head home at different times. Big leads, time left, weather, turnovers and traffic can send people home. A close upset or a comeback can keep them watching. Fans who left for good stay gone.
- **Where are the visitors?** A new home-date plan weighs the visiting school's following, nearby fans, travel and demand for home tickets. Its main section can stretch across nearby blocks with a visiting-heavy center, mixed edges and pockets of home colors. Other visitors sit in groups of one to eight, often two to four together. Existing empty seats stay empty. Bands keep their place. Recognized military and academy sections keep their uniforms and stay through normal crowd changes.
- **Can I see an earlier crowd?** Instant Replay, automatic clips and postgame highlights can use the crowd from that play. An old highlight can show seats that are empty now. Pausing holds movement. Normal and Fast Super Sim follow changes in score and clock. Slow Sim follows action on the field. A matching Midgame Save can pick up its recorded crowd. With no matching record, the mod estimates what it can.
- **Will the crowd be the same next time?** The same saved Dynasty matchup normally keeps its turnout, visiting share and seat plan. Older plans keep their layout. Use a fresh matchup or **Regenerate Next Crowd** to try a new plan. Regeneration applies once to the next newly started Dynasty game. Reloading a game in progress does not use it. The same inputs can give the same result.

## Hear the game change

A small crowd can give a simple cheer. A packed stadium can build a louder roar as a drive moves downfield. Chants and background sound follow the number of people in their seats. A big lead can calm the background. A visiting touchdown can hush the home fans.

At a home stadium, an **added positive visiting cheer needs at least 20% visiting fans** among the modeled supporters present. With 10,000 present, 1,999 visitors fall short and 2,000 reach the mark. Bands are left out of that count. If the mod cannot verify that the count is current and belongs to this game, it skips the cheer for that play. Fans arriving later cannot bring it back. Home disappointment, home-background quieting, camera effects and the game's own crowd voices have separate rules. A neutral site has a shared crowd.

| Soundscape choice | Starts at | What you can hear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crowd Sound Effects | On | Follows the seated crowd and adds reactions. Restart MMC and the game after changing this master switch. |
| Extra Crowd Energy | On | Adds roars, swells and stronger reactions when enough people are present. |
| Opening Atmosphere | On | Adds energy at the opener and early play selection. A big game or a showcase rivalry can get a stronger start. |
| Play Selection Atmosphere | On | Builds noise while you pick a play. Important downs and close finishes can get louder. |
| Overtime Atmosphere | On | Adds energy between overtime plays, kicks and two-point tries. |
| Upset Watch Sounds | On | From Q2 on, a home underdog challenging a ranked visitor can wake the crowd. Either underdog can qualify at a neutral site. |
| Live Play Reactions | On | Catches, long runs, scrambles, sacks, takeaways and kick returns can draw a reaction. |
| Skill Move and Big Hit Reactions | On | Adds sounds for moves and big hits. Trucks, hurdles, stiff arms and some big stops can add a little shake. Uses Live Play Reactions. |
| Buildup During Passes | On | Lets a sound swell while a qualifying pass is in the air. It settles after the catch or miss. Uses Live Play Reactions. |
| Cheers and Disappointment | On | Adds cheers or letdowns for big gains, stops, kicks and mistakes according to the side and its supporters. |
| Major Celebrations | On | Reserves the biggest reactions for moments such as an early touchdown, late winner, overtime win or game-sealing takeaway. |
| Crowd Volume Adjustment | 100% | Choose 0–100%. Lower values keep the mix closer to the game's usual volume. |

## Choose the camera and replays

Turn on the game's **Dynamic Camera Effects** to see added shake. Crowd size, the play and the camera all matter. Recorded clips stay steady. The game still controls which clips exist and you can skip a replay normally.

| Broadcast choice | Starts at | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show Fullness in Pause Menu | On | See the percentage of seats filled beside Resume. A dash means the reading is still getting ready. |
| Added Camera Shake | On | Master switch for the mod's added camera movement. |
| Away Team Reaction Shake | On | Lets a visiting crowd shake the camera on a qualifying play. Turn it off to keep eligible sound without that movement. |
| Wide Player Camera Compensation | Off | Adds extra correction when a very wide player camera makes shake hard to see. |
| Neutral-Site Crowd Shake | 75% | Choose 0–100% for either side. Cannon shake has its own setting. |
| Normal Play Selection Shake / Important Down Shake | 5% / 10% | Each takes 0–100%. Important downs include close third and fourth downs. |
| Live Play Shake / Stadium Cannon Shake | 11% / 18% | Each takes 0–30%. A cannon can keep its sound at zero shake. |
| Touchdown Shake / Major Celebration Shake | 16% / 22.5% | Each takes 0–100%. Sets the feel after the first impact. |
| Standard Camera Shake / Zoom Camera Shake / All-22 Camera Shake / Wide Camera Shake / Broadcast Camera Shake | 100% each | Set each view separately from 0–200%. |
| Additional Replays | On | Lets the mod ask for extra clips. You can still get the game's usual replays with this off. |
| Full-Screen Big Play Replays | On | Asks for a big-play clip. **Big Play Replay Minimum Yards** starts at **12** and takes **10–60**. Both replay switches must be on. A supported Broadcast Universe setup can receive that request. |
| Small Replays During Play Selection | On | An ordinary run or catch can get a small replay while you pick a play. Slow Sim has no play-selection window. |
| Defensive Play Replays | On | Adds chances for sack and tackle-for-loss clips alongside takeaway highlights. |

The three replay types use **Additional Replays**. Kneels and spikes do not get ordinary-play replay requests. Touchdown replays and celebration scenes belong to the game. A qualifying play can still go without a clip.

## Special games

- **Neutral sites and postseason.** On supported neutral Dynasty dates, including recognized championship games, the game sets turnout. Both schools can have a large section. The planned split stays between 30% and 70% per side. Postseason crowds have more reason to stay to the finish.
- **Dynasty Extensions spring games.** A supported spring exhibition uses the actual school's fans and colors, including recognized TeamBuilder teams. The stand-in opponent has no visiting section. The home school's following, venue, time and weather can affect turnout, up to 60%. Some fans take breaks. Others leave in the second half as the exhibition goes on, whatever the score. A poor series by the starting offense can qualify for a mild reaction.
- **Basic offline Play Now.** A fresh game with arrivals on starts with 75% of the available crowd seated. Fans can arrive and leave, react to plays and shake the camera. Play Now uses the game's normal visiting colors and has no Dynasty travel plan or saved crowd history.
- **Limits.** Online games, ordinary practice and general Road to Glory play are outside the supported live modes. Stadium views, exact championship layouts, new visiting cheers, audible spring boos and live frame rate still need in-game checks. A sound request alone cannot tell you what came through your speakers.

## More choices in the settings file

The in-game menu covers the main choices. **DynamicGamedayAtmosphere.ini** also lets you tune the smaller details below. Its comments give the units and allowed ranges for each value.

| What you can tune | Starting choices |
| --- | --- |
| Expected turnout for a new regular-season home game | On. Switch off the new turnout estimate to use the game's crowd size. |
| How quickly people leave | The normal per-quarter limit is 45 points of the original crowd. An ordinary step is 4 points. Seat changes wait for a break in play. |
| Comeback patience | A team that trailed by 21 and claws back 14 can keep more fans. A fading rally has 60 game seconds of grace on 12-minute quarters. |
| Late departures | The Q4 two-minute check starts on. A late score can send up to 5 extra points of the original crowd home. A severe traffic rush can allow 10 extra points beyond the quarter limit. A costly turnover can send up to 10 points home. |
| Delays and halftime | Late arrivals usually finish 75% through Q1. The fallback bad-weather delay is 5 points of the planned crowd. Up to 25% of fans taking a halftime break can stay away after a bad half. |
| What counts as a long play | The sound can react at 20 yards for a long completion at the catch, or 15 total yards on a run or catch-and-run. Each takes 10–60 yards. |
| Extra energy in full stands | A delayed second cheer on major plays, packed big-game reactions, packed-house buildup and rising energy on a good drive all start on. The big-game fullness floor starts at 55%. The extra boost for a dense crowd starts at 30%. |
| Stadium Pulse and opening shake | A 50% fullness setting helps decide when to allow the game's Stadium Pulse shake. Zero leaves that decision to the game. The lineup cheer starts at 6% shake and a loud opening series at up to 10%. |
| Catch and play shake | Catch impact starts at 12%, normal play fade at 5 seconds and the extra impact time at 1 second. |
| Celebration shake | A touchdown starts with 20% impact over a whole effect of 11 seconds. A major celebration starts with 30% impact and a 16-second effect. Their later strength has separate menu sliders. |
| Reports | The usual event-log limit is 2 MB per file, adjustable from 1–16 MB. Extra debug detail starts off. The plain status report gives a short summary and the detailed log can help trace one play. |
