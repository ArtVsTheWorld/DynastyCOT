**Ace’s Extra Settings 0.2.12**
Changes since public release 0.2.10.

MMC copies on the same Windows account now share your settings. The report explains which controls are ready and where a save failed. Your per-Dynasty clocks and four playcall rules carry forward.

Close MMC and the game, then copy ExtraSettings.dll from the standalone ZIP into MMC’s Plugins folder. The ZIP includes the full feature list and an illustrated player guide.
