**Ace’s Expanded Practice 0.8.24**
Changes since public release 0.8.21.

Drive Practice and Scrimmage can share the recognized Dynasty Extensions pregame controller screen. The report distinguishes waiting for practice from an active session. Both offline practice routes remain supported.

Close MMC and the game, then copy ExpandedPractice.dll from the standalone ZIP into MMC’s Plugins folder. The ZIP includes the full feature list and an illustrated player guide.
