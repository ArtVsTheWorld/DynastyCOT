**Ace’s Beyond the Field 1.0.24**
Changes since public release 1.0.7.

Choose separate grade scales, read position-specific Notes and see signings credited to the school the player joined. Settings and published editions travel between MMC copies on the same Windows account.

Close MMC and the game, then copy BeyondTheField.dll from the standalone ZIP into MMC’s Plugins folder. The ZIP includes the full feature list and an illustrated player guide.
