# Dynasty COT 1.0

**By Ace.** Four optional modules in one installer and one **Dynasty COT** entry in MMC. Choose the features you want.

## What's included

- **Beyond the Field:** Dynasty Social Media, recruiting notes and scouting fog, team and player rating styles, optional rating colors, compact money displays, and team-based Player Analytics. Analytics uses mod-calculated PFF-style grades from available Dynasty statistics, not official PFF grades. Only completed weeks contribute.
- **Gameday Atmosphere:** crowds that arrive, react and leave; visiting supporters shaped by travel and matchup appeal; crowd sound and camera shake; and in-game controls for your preferred atmosphere. Visiting Fan Strength affects new plans without rewriting saved crowd history.
- **Expanded Practice:** full drives and scrimmages, side switching, starting spots and session statistics in offline Dynasty or main-menu Practice. Normal practice stays available.
- **Extra Settings:** separate offensive and defensive play clocks, playcall limits and cooldowns, saved independently for each Dynasty.

## Final 1.0 improvements

- Fixed Current NIL labels reverting to base-game values after edits. Compact dollar labels, native editing values and budget arithmetic stay separate.
- Fixed brief numerical rating flashes, incorrect bucket labels and the missing selection highlight on the Ratings tab. Descriptive grades use capitals, fit their rating circles and support matching bar colors.
- Reduced unnecessary work when entering Ratings or returning from cards opened through Analytics. Recruiting money adapters are isolated so an unused native class cannot disable the others.
- Kept Dynasty fog, rating styles, colors and money formatting out of the main menu and Play Now, without resetting saved Dynasty preferences.
- Added basic offline Play Now crowd sound, shake, arrivals and departures. With arrivals enabled, fresh games open at 75% of the available original crowd, then continue filling. Visiting-fan appearance stays native; Dynasty travel/season plans and saved crowd history are not used.
- Kept Additional Replays for eligible non-touchdown plays. COT leaves touchdown replays and celebrations to the game's native presentation while the skipped-sequence issue is investigated further.
- Branded runtime DLL names match the module names. Beyond the Field and Gameday Atmosphere load directly; Extra Settings and Expanded Practice keep their verified helpers. Existing data folders and compatibility IDs remain intact.
- Simplified installation with automatic Mod Manager detection, optional modules, selected/full removal and transaction rollback. Module descriptions explain the features; logs stay in module folders.

## Install or update

1. Download **DynastyCOT-Setup-1.0.0.exe** from this release.
2. Close the game and MMC Mod Manager.
3. Run setup, confirm the detected folder containing **MMCModManager.exe**, choose your modules and install.
4. Restart MMC and launch the game normally. Keep your previous installer and save backups.

Setup preserves settings and published history. Use **Undo changes** to restore an installation backup. Do not enable older standalone copies alongside COT. The ZIP is also provided for the documented manual/update workflow.

## Verification and compatibility

Ace installed this final 1.0 package, restarted MMC and the game, and reported the fixes working. Read-only checks matched all six installed binary hashes to the release package and confirmed both Beyond the Field natives and the Gameday Atmosphere native loaded. This is targeted live confirmation, not certification of every game/overhaul combination or a new FPS benchmark.

Automated checks include all 16 module combinations, 116 installer checks, 135 legacy-runtime migration checks, 123 same-version upgrade checks, 36 setup GUI/engine checks, 95 updater checks, 665 native scope/history/replay-exclusion checks and 1,669 Dynasty presentation-scope checks. Settings, published accounts/posts/history and crowd-history preservation are covered by fixtures.

AIO 1.4.1 and its TeamBuilder patch have targeted adapters. Unknown native layouts and foreign hook owners remain rejected. Analytics remains team-only; its nonworking Player Stats shortcut is hidden. Online modes are not supported. See the [verification report](https://github.com/ArtVsTheWorld/DynastyCOT/blob/main/docs/release/verification-1.0.0.md) and [regression checklist](https://github.com/ArtVsTheWorld/DynastyCOT/blob/main/LIVE-ACCEPTANCE.md) for the practical limits and remaining broader checks.

Updates remain manual and optional. You can keep an older release and restore installation backups; rollback does not roll back a Dynasty save.
