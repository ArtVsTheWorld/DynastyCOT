**Gameday Atmosphere — Update 4**

**Attendance & Visiting Fans**
- Reworked turnout around program support, recent seasons and stadium capacity. TeamBuilder teams use the available program information rather than made-up past records.
- Visiting support now considers fanbase demand, travel distance and matchup appeal. Big fanbases travel better; strong home support helps prevent a takeover.
- Smaller, less uniform visiting sections, with home fans mixed in and traveling fans sitting in little groups.
- New Visiting Fan Strength setting. 100% keeps the normal model; changes affect new crowd plans. Existing plans and crowd history are kept.
- Home and visiting fans leave at different rates. Winning visitors still thin during a blowout, but more slowly than the losing home crowd.
- Bands and verified military crowd groups stay protected.

**Sound, Shake & Presentation**
- Shake requests matching crowd sound, including pre-play buildup.
- Background roar settles as the home deficit grows. Cheers and scoring reactions take priority over that background noise.
- Added replays remain available for non-touchdown plays. Touchdowns use the game's normal presentation while we investigate skipped celebrations.
- Gentler opening-kickoff shake and better first-drive atmosphere.
- Basic offline Play Now crowd reactions, sound and shake. Fresh games start at 75% with arrivals enabled; Dynasty travel/history stay separate.

**Fixes & Installation**
- Better crowd-plan persistence and save/resume matching, plus fixes for own-team fumble recoveries being mistaken for takeaways.
- Direct runtime loading from MMC, with no separate Gameday loader EXE.
- Included in **Dynasty COT 1.0** as an optional module. Close the game and MMC, run setup, and choose what you want.

Settings and history are retained. As always, exact game/overhaul combinations still need testing.
