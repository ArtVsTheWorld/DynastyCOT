# Dynasty COT 0.1.11 private test candidate

Beyond the Field gets a reviewed editorial refresh and exactly 150 new variants. This candidate is built from source for local testing. It is not published or installed into live MMC by the build process.

## Changes since 0.1.10

- Imported selected checkpoint-09 rewrites after correcting attribution, sentence joins, timing, units and rendered-length paths. Retained clearer original wording, player/personality tags, rivalry humor, named-poll context, uncertainty and projection labels where the proposal weakened them.
- Added 120 structurally different alternatives across 20 frequently displayed families, plus 30 short factual forms and context-aware replies. Fans retain humor, frustration, excitement and rivalry pettiness. Reporters, analysts, officials and restrained players keep their established roles and persistent identities.
- Repetition memory/refinement now follows the actual native displayed windows, including up to 250 National cards. It no longer assumes the retired 30/25/60/100 limits. Immutable history projections and structural matches are cached without changing selection results.
- Recruit game posts use the native label **COT HS Game**. Social Media's setting description explains that these use persistent mod-generated high school results, separate from native records. Posts keep their source metadata; they do not expose development traits or alter native stats.
- No higher post budgets, display caps, extra week pages or new modules. Fifteen routine roots per team, protected major news, recruit-statline exemptions and draft coverage remain in place. Four modules remain independently selectable. Analytics, fog, money displays, crowds, practice and Extra Settings retain their existing behavior.

Existing published wording, accounts, saves, settings and history are preserved. The refresh applies to new publications. No save migration or hot-reload prototype is included. The experimental Social Media list/sidebar path stays disabled.

## Evidence and limitations

The comparison uses 31 recorded editions and six explicit acceptance fixtures, including WMU's 31–26 upset of #14 Michigan, sparse context, long names, recruit records, postseason and offseason. Generated post and primary-story counts are unchanged; no protected baseline event is lost. National adjacent near-duplicates fall sharply, but the broader National multi-edition repetition metric improves modestly and does not meet a 50% reduction. Mandatory high-volume news still exhausts supported angles.

See [verification and limits](../verification-0.1.11.md). Generation timings are offline timings, not live menu/FPS measurements. Exact game/AIO/TeamBuilder compatibility and live performance remain pending acceptance.

## Install and rollback

Close the game and MMC, keep the 0.1.10 installer, then run `DynastyCOT-Setup-0.1.11-private.exe`. Confirm the detected MMC Mod Manager folder and choose your modules. Updates remain user controlled. Use **Backups** to restore the prior payload, or reinstall 0.1.10. Settings, account identities and publication history are retained during install/update/rollback. Rollback does not erase new publications or rewind your game save.

Test copied DYNASTY-EPUFT checkpoints. Do not overwrite originals. Start with a busy regular-season National view, then offseason draft/signings, recruiting edits and preseason polls. Verify old editions retain their wording, new editions refresh consistently, and actual menu entry/scroll time and FPS remain reasonable.
