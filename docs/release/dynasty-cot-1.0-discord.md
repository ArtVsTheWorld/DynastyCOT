**DYNASTY COT 1.0 by Ace**

Four optional modules, one installer. Pick what you want:

**Beyond the Field**
- Dynasty Social Media with news, reactions, recruiting and more.
- Recruiting Notes, scouting fog, letter/word ratings, optional rating colors and clearer money labels.
- Team Player Analytics with weekly and season PFF-style grades calculated from available game stats. These are mod grades, not official PFF grades.

**Gameday Atmosphere**
- Fans arrive, cheer, travel and leave as the matchup develops.
- More varied visiting sections, crowd sound and camera shake, plus Visiting Fan Strength controls.
- Basic offline Play Now support. Fresh crowds start at 75% with arrivals on.

**Expanded Practice**
- Full drives and scrimmages in offline Dynasty or main-menu Practice, with side switching and session stats.

**Extra Settings**
- More play clocks, playcall limits and cooldowns. Each Dynasty keeps its own choices.

**Final fixes**
NIL labels stay formatted after edits. Ratings no longer flash numbers; descriptive grades fit and use capitals. Player-card navigation does less extra work. Dynasty fog stays out of Play Now/main-menu screens. Added replays remain available for non-touchdown plays; touchdowns use native presentation.

**Install/update:** Close the game and MMC, run setup, confirm the detected Mod Manager folder and choose your modules. Settings and published history are kept. Undo changes restores an installation backup. Disable old standalone copies.

Offline modes only. AIO/TeamBuilder have targeted support; other combinations still need testing.
