# Dynasty COT 0.1.14-private

Private test candidate, built from integration source. Not installed or published.

Beyond the Field's Player Analytics now grades accumulated released production instead of averaging compressed weekly grades. Position-relative calibration gives every position room for strong and poor grades, with ties and small samples respected. OL grades use recorded sacks allowed and participation; sparse pancakes no longer control upside. Kickers and punters use attempts rather than unrelated participation as their production denominator. Native half-sacks retain their correct weighting.

Lower-prestige programs receive wider, deterministic grading uncertainty; higher-prestige programs receive less. Prestige does not grant a positive or negative quality adjustment. Missing prestige uses a neutral uncertainty level. Ratings and development traits do not enter grades.

New PFF posts use the same compiled model as the menu: PFF / @PFF_College, with the native `bticl_win64_P_color` icon. There are 32 variants across weekly grades, season spotlights, lower-grade watches and meaningful grade movements. At most four optional roots per edition; existing team budgets, major-news protections and Social National's 250-card limit remain. Busy news editions can omit PFF rather than displace essential news. No automatic generic reaction threads are added.

Published wording, accounts/history, settings, native stats and saves are preserved. Updating the derived grading model recalculates Analytics estimates for available historical weeks; it does not rewrite old published posts. Current-week grades remain hidden until a later week/stage, and Season includes only released games.

See the [audit and verification](../player-analytics-0.1.14.md) for the actual sim comparison, performance distinction and limitations. These are COT production estimates, not PFF's proprietary film grades or actual reports from PFF. No affiliation is implied. In particular, native data does not support complete OL run-blocking, coverage or pressure evaluation.

## Testing and rollback

Close the game and MMC, install this version using your chosen modules, then restart. Keep 0.1.13's installer or use the installer's Backups screen to restore the previous payload. User data is preserved; rollback never rewinds a Dynasty save.

1. Check Analytics Season and several prior weeks for OL, QB, defensive players, K and P. Compare stronger and weaker production, not overall ratings. Current week must remain absent.
2. Switch weeks/teams and sort. Open a full player card and Back; check identity, selection, Fog and native colors. National Analytics and View Player Stats remain unavailable as in 0.1.13.
3. Advance a disposable checkpoint copy, then check PFF in Social's Analysis category and appropriate scopes. Grades must agree with that player's matching released week or Season, with units shown correctly.
4. Reopen/refresh and reload the Dynasty: grades and published PFF text should remain stable. Test another Dynasty for isolation. Check old posts remain unchanged.

Fresh live menu/FPS, gameplay and exact overhaul acceptance remain pending. This candidate adds no hooks or live hot-reload prototype, and startup/offline checks are not live compatibility certification.
