practice configuration byte 1, and the same exact native match/state vtables
as Dynasty practice (mode 14). The previous helper deliberately accepted only
14/3. No fingerprints or native addresses needed changing.

Its native guards recognize both explicit modes and retain their existing
offline, bound-match, caller and configuration checks. They do not admit
online modes, Play Now, or other game modes. One hot trampoline has exactly
1,024 bytes; the size assertion and executable tests protect its slot boundary.
No executable/helper verification or native hook ownership checks were removed.

Verification: 154 mode/persistence/scoring checks, the existing drive, CPU,
scrimmage, startup, first-down and diagnostic suites, plus 7,445 actual x64 hook
execution checks in each of the Dynasty and main-menu fixture contexts passed.
The native manifest was regenerated without altering the native fingerprints.

For live acceptance, the inactive installed helper was stopped cooperatively.

installed binaries were not overwritten. Its live log recorded correct play
completion, gain, down/distance, next-play selection and restoration of Default
Practice. Ace confirmed Drive Practice, Scrimmage, HUD and returning to Default
all behaved correctly. The existing Beyond the Field live fixes were untouched.

Exiting to Play Now restored all practice hooks successfully. Still to check:
re-entering practice, a full possession turnover and returning to Dynasty
practice after this change. The source-built 1.0 installer includes it.

## Gameday Atmosphere

The loaded Northwestern/Notre Dame Play Now fixture uses mode 17, session 3,
configuration flag 0 and the exact same native match/state vtables. This flag
distinguishes an actual game from main-menu practice. All three values are
required, in addition to the existing type, ownership and fingerprint checks.

The new basic path admits the live score, clock, weather, neutral venue,
rivalry and native UI team rankings. Existing live-match crowd departure,
sound, measured-fullness and camera rules can use that context. Configuration
flag 1 or an unknown flag is rejected. Online sessions remain excluded.

EA visitor appearance remains native. With arrivals enabled, newly generated Play Now plans open at 75% of the original available crowd, independent of rank, weather, rivalry and neutral-site modifiers. The existing arrival curve continues from that opening; disabling arrivals retains native fullness. An already running match is not reset. Dynasty opening rules are unchanged.

Dynasty launch capture, audience foundations, travel plans and their persisted histories retain their Dynasty scope. Play Now uses an in-memory plan and does not resume or append a Dynasty attendance archive. Replay playback suspends its live atmosphere without borrowing recorded crowds from Dynasty.

The clean source build loaded in Texas–Oklahoma neutral/rivalry Play Now. Ace confirmed added roar/cheers and camera shake after kickoff and several snaps. Crowd arrivals progressed, and the three persisted Dynasty crowd/history files retained identical hashes. No sound/camera callback sample exceeded 4 ms in that trace. One-time seating preparation reached 226 ms and applying the crowd reached 250 ms; these observations do not establish zero stutter or FPS performance. The subsequent 75% opening policy still needs a new-game live test.

Verification now includes production-reader/type, history-isolation and touchdown-policy hook tests. Both home/away scoring, delayed score confirmation, native touchdown predicates, new snaps, world/epoch changes and simulated/unknown results are covered. Ordinary completed non-scoring plays retain eligible added replay comparisons. No native offsets, signatures or fingerprints were relaxed.

No replay setting or crowd history is migrated.

## Dynasty-only Beyond the Field presentation

The Dynasty hub can report mode -1, not just 14. Admission therefore also requires offline session 3, an exact owned career service, its active flag, a valid LeagueID and the matching loaded profile. Main-menu/Play Now reads receive native display options; saved settings are unchanged. New display-program adapters are not attached outside the Dynasty scope, while previously owned definitions can still run their Off/restoration path. Creation-menu settings use the raw saved choices and are not accidentally masked.

Production options/store tests cover 1,669 scope, preference and persistence cases. Read-only samples confirmed the Dynasty hub at mode -1/session 3 with an owned active career and its valid LeagueID. Returning to the main menu kept mode -1/session 3 but cleared the active flag and LeagueID; the new guard rejects that state. Final clean-build menu/fog checks remain live acceptance work; these input observations and offline tests are not proof of rendered game compatibility.

The earlier Play Now trace also recorded an unavailable in-game player-card adapter bank with its expected body hash (not a changed-game fingerprint). The new scope avoids first-time card-adapter attachment outside Dynasty. Its behavior in a Dynasty game remains a live review item; no fingerprint was bypassed to silence the warning.
