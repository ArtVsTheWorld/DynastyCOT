# Dynasty COT 0.1.15-private

Private source-built test candidate. No live install or publication.

- Incorporated the supplied ChatGPT PFF revision: 48 base variants (5 retained, 27 rewritten, 16 added), plus all 15 supplied ranking/honor templates. Total: 63 variants across nine PFF families. Unsupported dash glyphs use ordinary punctuation.
- Added qualified season Top 3 boards by position, Offensive/Defensive Player of the Week and Offense/Defense of the Week. These are COT/PFF grade-board honors; they do not create native game awards or change simulation data.
- Social's National display limit is now **300 total cards, including replies**, with native/JS parity. Important-news and draft protections, category-first filtering, thread ancestry, compact numbering and one edition per week remain.

PFF retains its existing identity, handle and requested native icon. New copy applies to new publications; published wording/history, saves, settings and all four independently selectable modules are preserved. The 0.1.14 grading model is unchanged.

PFF remains capped at four optional roots per edition. Weekly player and team-unit honors alternate, with a changed position board and program post sharing the remaining slots. Incomplete data, insufficient qualifying samples, ambiguous ties or busy news budgets can omit a post. The allowance is a maximum, not a quota.

See the [integration/verification report](../pff-editorial-0.1.15.md) for qualification, unit aggregation, ties, examples and limitations. Grades are COT estimates, not actual PFF film grades/reporting; no affiliation is implied. Source/offline checks do not certify live performance or overhaul compatibility.

## Test and rollback

Close MMC/game and install the versioned candidate, then restart. Keep the 0.1.14 installer or use Backups to restore the prior payload. Settings/history are preserved; rollback does not rewind a Dynasty save.

1. Advance a disposable checkpoint copy to a new publication. In Social's Analysis category, inspect PFF position boards and weekly player/unit honors when eligible. Confirm exact schools, grades and source week against Analytics. Existing same-week PFF posts keep their published wording.
2. Reopen/reload: identities, selected honors and wording must remain stable. Check another Dynasty for isolation.
3. In a busy National edition, verify compact `x/300` numbering, category/scope changes, intact replies and all 224 draft picks when available. One week must remain one edition. Observe actual scrolling/FPS with the larger view.
4. Recheck team-only Analytics, current-week hiding, full Card/Back and Fog; this candidate adds no new Analytics navigation or grading formula.

Live installation, menu/FPS, exact AIO/overhaul and gameplay acceptance remain pending. No new hooks or external helpers were added.
