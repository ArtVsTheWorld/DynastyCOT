# Source references

The read-only betting model in `Tools/betting_model.mjs` uses the starter/depth blend and league-relative gain approach from Balla's CFB27 Team Overall Calculation Tool, Option A (v0.1), as implemented in Ace's CFB Toolkit's Smart Force Win. It uses an independently simplified matchup/spread calculation and does not invoke force-win actions or change game outcomes.

Reference: https://github.com/ArtVsTheWorld/Aces-CFB-Toolkit/tree/main/src/main/tools/forceWin/core
