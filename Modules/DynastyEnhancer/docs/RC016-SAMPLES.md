# Readable before/after examples

The upset comparison below generates fresh posts from identical synthetic normalized inputs reproducing the verified W. Michigan31–26 win over#14Michigan. These are not edited copies of published history. Account names are illustrative fictional accounts; the user’s account identities and stored posts are retained.

## W. Michigan at Michigan

The original generated conversation includes:

> Reporter: An upset victory for Unranked W. Michigan, with #14 Michigan on the other side of a 31–26 final.
>
> Winning-side reply: That's a result worth sitting with for a minute.

The candidate keeps the factual result and adds supported, different perspectives:

> Losing-side fan: Losing to W. Michigan,31–26, with that ranking beside our name. There isn't a comforting way to arrange it.
>
> Michigan State rival: No analysis from me. Just the W. Michigan–Michigan result and a smile.
>
> Analyst: Judge W. Michigan's performance by the opponent it actually beat:#14Michigan. The program gap adds context.

A captured Michigan–Michigan State rivalry is required for that rival perspective. Ranking, score, and program-strength inputs are verified; there is no invented interview, turnover explanation, injury, or coaching transaction. Ordinary story counts and conversation limits still apply. An upgrade replay of the fixture retained all seven original stored post objects unchanged.

The improvement is incomplete: the primary reporter wording and some legacy generic reactions can still be selected. The example intentionally retains that limitation rather than substituting a hand-picked ideal headline.

## Access to stored news

Previously, a Fan category could look empty when its only eligible fan contribution replied to a reporter. It now displays that actual reporter parent as context, followed by the matching fan reply. Category filtering happens before paging. For a busy National Recruiting edition with9,300 eligible cards, the first page contains100 cards; the publication dropdown offers the next page while the stamps distinguish displayed and eligible counts.
