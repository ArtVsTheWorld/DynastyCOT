## Recap and stat integrity

Quarter totals must reconcile with the published final. Overtime must follow tied regulation and reconcile with the final score. Supported narratives include fourth-quarter comebacks, halftime rallies, surviving late pressure, second-half shutdowns and pulling away late. Invalid/missing quarters fall back to corroborated box-score detail without inventing drives, clocks or game-winning plays. Quarter data describes the score at a boundary, not a possession-by-possession account.

Offensive stat lines include meaningful passing/rushing or rushing/receiving production. Signed yardage fields can corroborate negative-yardage games; counting stats remain nonnegative. This restores legitimate season rushing totals previously suppressed by one negative rushing game. Public player references still include natural position labels before 280-character/512-byte validation.
