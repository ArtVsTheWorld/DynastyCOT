# v75: matchups, fan voices and season endings

## Opponent comparisons

An edition-stable 40% fixture gate makes the comparisons occasional. Selected current-week previews/recent recaps can compare passing and rushing production against the opposing defense, including yards per attempt/carry when both samples support it, or protection against the opposing pass rush. Each side needs at least three earlier published games and a corroborated overall record. Recap baselines strictly exclude the game being recapped. Missing fields suppress the particular comparison.

Receiver/secondary stories use published, unique, season-corroborated player-game records. They compare a productive WR/TE with a productive CB/safety on the opposing unit; they do not claim shadow coverage, a matchup assignment or that one defender allowed that receiver's yards. Recaps give independent lines from the same actual fixture. Older-phase caches/performances remain available for season-wide postseason comparisons, with future/unpublished games excluded.

Previews appear under Upcoming and recaps under Recaps, as well as their account categories. National top-60 editorial assignment remains; the school beat can cover the same facts for smaller programs.

## Actual season endings

| Situation | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Before configured conference championship week | No season-ending claims. |
| Actual future assigned game | No final season farewell. |
| Six or more wins, awaiting a bowl assignment | Pending, even if the schedule is temporarily empty. |
| Five wins during title week | Also pending until postseason scheduling. |
| Fewer than five wins after a complete regular slate | Can publish the season review in championship week. |
| Ordinary bowl completed / playoff loss | Season complete when records corroborate and no assigned game remains. |
| Playoff win before national final | No completed-season/perfect-season claim. |
| National final completed | Final season review, including a perfect-season reaction if no losses/ties. |
| National-title phase, regular-only team still without a postseason assignment | Can close its completed regular-only season. |

The exact bowl-selection publication week remains unverified; the user is testing it. No fixed selection week is invented. Completed records require at least eight published games, correct wins/losses/ties and no assigned unplayed fixture. Missing/unpublished results suppress the ending.

An edition ledger records the first supported season-ending publication. Same-week refreshes retain it; later weeks/phases do not repeat it. An unfinished restored state clears a stale ledger entry. The review includes team thanks, separate Barstool/After Dark reactions, player gratitude and senior farewell language, a media season review, prior-season comparison, and optional native recruiting-class/job-security reports. Seniors currently redshirting are not automatically given a final-season farewell. No draft declaration, retirement, firing or future coaching hire is invented.

Poor-season fan accountability can name the HC/OC/DC, using the observed scoring profile to pick the staff subject. A QB discussion needs a verified meaningful attempt sample with at least eight INT and INT >= passing TD; it cites those actual figures. Hot-seat/low-security reporting uses the native coach status. Recruiting-class ranks use native ordering, not a made-up star formula, and are labeled current rather than final.

Winless full regular-season finishes receive sober wording across accounts. A perfect full-season finish receives distinctly celebratory wording. An unbeaten regular season awaiting the playoff/bowl does not qualify as a completed perfect season.
