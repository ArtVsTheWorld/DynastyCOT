Native historical totals already include the current season's results. The reader does not add current wins again. Appearance and title counts are independent: older poll-era national titles and regular-season conference titles legitimately exist without title-game appearances. TeamBuilder all-time totals can differ from inherited season-series history; neither source is silently corrected or replaced with an invented history.

Milestones seed quietly when v74 first observes a dynasty. An established baseline persists through same-week refreshes and earlier-phase history, so a new achievement remains in its publication edition without duplicating. A restored earlier state suppresses crossings no longer supported by current totals. Installing v74 does not claim every older career achievement happened this week.

The historical first-round bowl field also stores ordinary bowls before and after CFP began. It alone cannot establish a playoff appearance. That requires an explicit CFPFirstRound presentation or a post-2013 quarterfinal/semifinal/national title result. Conference-title appearance dates cannot be reconstructed reliably from a title-winner flag alone; those dates are omitted. Native all-time appearance-count increments remain supported.

## Advanced analysis

Twenty-two additional scenarios cover:

- Broken tackles per 100 rushing attempts; yards after first contact per carry; YAC as a share of receiving yards; recorded drops relative to catches plus drops; receiving production by a running back.
- Player tackles, sacks, TFL, INT, passes defended, forced fumbles and recoveries per appearance.
- OL sacks allowed per 100 **offensive** snaps, including run plays; pancakes per game. Pass-block-only snap counts are unavailable in these records.
- Field goals from 40-49 and 50-plus yards, kickoff touchbacks, punts inside the 20 with touchbacks, kick and punt return average/TD.
- Recent single-game QB passing plus rushing and RB rushing plus receiving lines.

Season numerators and denominators must exactly match the sum of unique published player-game records, with the player's games played matching those records. Missing or conflicting data suppresses the rate. Derived offensive/defensive returner records are supported without adding duplicate base rows. Broken tackles are not called a percentage of carries: more than one can occur on a run. YAC greater than net receiving yards is not clamped into a misleading percentage. Catch-plus-drop rate is explicitly not a target rate. Made/attempt and punt-placement bounds are checked.

Rotation allows at most two new season angles per player and ten per team, with offense/defense/line/special-teams balance. CPU/user eligibility is identical. The existing national top-60 editorial policy applies; smaller-program analysis stays with school beats. All five named national analysts remain represented. Confirmed user-team postseason announcements receive curation space before fan reactions use their thread slots. Existing For You/National/conference display caps and descending likes/thread order remain.
