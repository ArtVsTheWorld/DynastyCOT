# Update 2

Working build **1.54.0** — source tag **v1.54.0**.

- **A more involved crowd:** Layered roars, swells and cheers build energy before the snap on home and away offense. Packed ordinary games can qualify too. Drives, close critical downs, goal-line threats, late winning drives and overtime get more anticipation.
- **More reactive big plays:** During-play responses for long catches, runs, scrambles, interceptions, forced fumbles and returns. Sacks, defensive stops, deep breakups, field goals and safeties also get situational reactions. Longer runs/returns take longer to settle after the whistle.
- **Bigger celebrations:** Expanded eruptions for early/long touchdowns, Hail Marys, late go-ahead scores, sealing takeaways, overtime winners and major upsets. Winning roars carry through postgame presentation until the stats/highlights menu.
- **Reworked camera shake:** Different, adjustable profiles for ordinary plays, critical downs, touchdowns and eruptions. Effects follow live camera cuts, soften on close-ups and leave recorded replays steady.
- Sound follows the rendered seating fullness. Fuller crowds get more presence; sparse crowds get calmer native reactions. Chants have a separate limit. Gameplay HFA remains unchanged.
- Two arrival groups: normal schools around 55%; UCLA, Miami, Georgia Tech and Maryland around 40%. Big ranked/postseason games start at least 60% of the game's planned crowd. Matchup/weather adjustments still apply; rivalry alone provides an opening-attendance bonus.
- Conference championships get playoff treatment. Smarter traffic departures and more patient temporary halftime breaks in close big games.
- A qualifying big-game opening kickoff starts with a cheer and carries roar/swell through the kick, return and whistle.
- Clearer INI descriptions, organized logs and fixes for crowd refills, missed reactions and cleanup.

The download is the approved original Update 2 package. The full feature list describes the settings, qualification rules and timing in detail.
