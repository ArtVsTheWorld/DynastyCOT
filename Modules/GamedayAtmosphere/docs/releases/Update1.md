# Update 1

Working build **1.26.1** — source tag **v1.26.1**.

- **More natural crowd distribution:** Denser seating near the field, gradually thinning upper sections, small clusters and stragglers. Sections fill and empty at different speeds. Arrivals, departures and replays share the same pattern.
- Higher default starting crowds, with a 35% opening minimum. Competitive matchups attract more early fans; ranking gaps reduce them.
- More fans arrive early, followed by a smaller wave of stragglers.
- Halftime permanent departures and temporary seat breaks respond to the game situation.
- Blowouts that develop late trigger stronger departures; noncompetitive postseason games get more Q4 exits.
- Improved comeback patience using clock, possession, timeouts and field position.
- Less dependence on how often play selection appears.
- Crowd updates work during Slow, Normal and Fast Super Sim.
- Improved pregame initialization, kickoff continuity, quit/reload, postgame crowds and the Midgame Save opening handoff.

The download is the original 1.26.1 package, not a rebuild.
