# Update 3 Hotfix
**Settings & Installation**
- Gameday Atmosphere is now in Pause > Options > Settings, after Auto-Subs, sharing your Dynasty/RTG settings
- 44 plainly explained settings, including separate shake sliders for all five cameras
- New ZIP layout: copy the DLL AND its same-named folder into MMC's Plugins folder with the game and MMC closed
- Separate loader, fixes for deeply nested settings paths and clearer launch errors
- Update 3 settings are kept, while Update 2 still gets the automatic one-time refresh

**Camera Shake**
- Shake now follows the actual lens/FOV, including modded cameras, rather than preset-based strength offsets
- Zoom keeps its accepted strength, with gentler Standard/wider framing
- All camera sliders default to 100, so return temporary 90% test settings to 100 after updating
- Away Team Reaction Shake is now 0-100%, default 0, with 100 matching the home reaction
- Neutral-Site Crowd Shake is 0-100% for both teams, defaulting to the usual 75%
- Trucks are 50% stronger, confirmed big hits match them on stops or losses, and immediately tackled big catches get a stronger impact
- Smoother touchdown/eruption shake through sound and celebration-camera handoffs, with gentler opening-kickoff shake
